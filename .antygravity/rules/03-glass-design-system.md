# Glass Design System

PrismUI is rooted in Glassmorphism.
- **Backdrop Blur**: Use `backdrop-blur-md` or `backdrop-blur-lg` heavily.
- **Backgrounds (Light Mode)**: Use `bg-white/30`, `bg-white/40`, or `bg-white/50`.
- **Backgrounds (Dark Mode)**: Use `dark:bg-slate-900/40`, `dark:bg-slate-900/60`, or `dark:bg-black/40`.
- **Borders**: Thin, semi-transparent borders to define structure without losing the glass effect: `border border-white/20` (light mode) and `dark:border-white/10` (dark mode).
- **Shadows**: Soft, diffuse shadows `shadow-lg` or `shadow-xl` to create depth.
- **Text**: Ensure high contrast for readability. Admin panels require clear data presentation. Use `text-slate-800` and `dark:text-slate-100`.
